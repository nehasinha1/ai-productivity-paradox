# Adoption Scoring Model — Methodology Notes

The `adoption_scoring_model.xlsx` workbook scores organizational AI readiness across 5 dimensions.
Each dimension is rated 1–5 and weighted by its empirical correlation with successful adoption outcomes.

## Dimensions & Weights

| Dimension | Weight | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Executive sponsorship | 25% | Is there a named executive owner for AI initiatives? |
| Workflow integration | 25% | Are tools embedded in daily work, or bolted on separately? |
| Training investment | 20% | Hours of structured AI training per employee per quarter |
| Measurement baseline | 15% | Was productivity measured *before* tool deployment? |
| Change management program | 15% | Formal OCM plan with milestones and adoption metrics |

## Scoring

**Total score 0–100:**
- 80–100: High readiness — likely to see gains within 6 months
- 60–79: Moderate — gains possible with targeted intervention
- 40–59: At risk — structural gaps likely to cause productivity dip without recovery
- Below 40: Low readiness — tool investment likely wasted without foundational work first

## Usage

Fill in columns B–F for each team or business unit. The model auto-calculates weighted scores
and plots a radar chart per unit for stakeholder presentations.
